# v3.3 Payroll Accuracy Audit (NHS shifts, Night Security rates, placements)


Branch `fix/v3.3-payroll-accuracy`, stacked on `release/v3.3.0-rc1`. **Not deployed.**

**Evidence:** the source code; real August 2026 Staffline payslip lines (fixtures in `Tests/Staffline_Tests.js`); and the **October 2026 Staffline payslip figures examined by the owner** (Night Security lines for w/e 04/10, 11/10 and 18/10; gross, deductions and net for w/e 11/10 and 18/10). The live PaySheet and calendar were not accessible from development.
## 1. NHS shift recognition: confirmed defect, fixed

**Before (RC):** NHS shifts were recognised only from title tokens for **06:00–14:00** ("6-2", "6am") and **14:00–22:00** ("2-10", "2pm"). **08:00–16:00 and 10:00–18:00 were never recognised.** Times written as "06:00-14:00" or "0600-1400" weren't read, and a timed event titled just "NHS Antrim" was never imported (only reported). "Causeway Hospital" or "Antrim Area Hospital" without "NHS" weren't treated as NHS.

**Now (`CalendarService.classifyNhsEvent`):**
- **Both hospitals.** The title contains "NHS" or "HSC" (as before), "Antrim Hospital", "Antrim Area Hospital" or "Causeway Hospital". "Coleraine" or "Causeway" *alone* is deliberately **not** NHS, because the council warden jobs use those names.
- **The shift window comes from the title** in any common format: 6-2, 8-4, 10-6, 2-10, 06:00-14:00, 0800-1600, 8am-4pm, 10:00–18:00 (en-dash), "8 to 4", 14:00 - 22:00. 12-hour shorthand is read the way NHS shifts run.
- **Otherwise from the event's own start and end** (timed, same-day, on the hour). This is skipped when the title names another role ("NHS Coleraine security") or states a different time ("NHS 9am"); those stay *reported, not guessed*. All-day events with no time stay unrecognised.
- **Only the four windows are paid.** Anything else (e.g. 07:00–15:00) is left for review.

| Window | Weekday | Saturday | Sunday |
|---|---|---|---|
| 06:00–14:00 | NHS 6am-2pm Basic **£95.33** | NHS Saturday **£134.40** | NHS Sunday **£174.45** |
| 08:00–16:00 | Basic **£95.33** | £134.40 | £174.45 |
| 10:00–18:00 | Basic **£95.33** | £134.40 | £174.45 |
| 14:00–22:00 | NHS 2pm-10pm Split **£105.75** | £134.40 | £174.45 |

Basis, verified on real payslips: every NHS shift is 8h with a 30-minute unpaid break, i.e. **7.5 paid hours** (Aug payslip, N621137: 28 basic + 9.5 unsocial = 37.5h for 5 shifts).
- 7.5 × £12.71 = £95.33.
- The 14–22 split is 5.5h basic + **2h unsocial (20:00–22:00) at £17.92** = £69.91 + £35.84 = £105.75. The unsocial rate is unchanged.
- Saturday and Sunday pay every window at the Saturday/Sunday rate, as the existing rules did.
- No new shift types are needed: 08–16 and 10–18 use the existing NHS **Basic** type, as the payslip does.

Hospital identity is kept on the Calendar Sync Record (event title). Calendar colours are not read or changed anywhere in Pay Tracker, so nothing alters them.

## 2. Night Security rates: effective-dated, never global

| Rate | Value | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| ×1.33 (Enhanced and Overtime) | £17.91 → **£17.92** | August 2026 payslips (N621105, N624148) | Correction: Staffline always paid £17.92 (£13.47 × 1.33 = £17.9151) |
| ×1.50 Enhanced, shifts up to **Sun 4 Oct 2026** | **£20.21** | w/e 04/10/2026: 4.50 × £20.21 = £90.95 | Historical rate, unchanged |
| ×1.50 Enhanced, shifts from **Mon 5 Oct 2026** | **£20.87** | w/e 11/10 and w/e 18/10: 8.00 × £20.87 = £166.96 | **Proven by payslips; contractual start date not confirmed** |
| Basic, ×1.20, ×2.00, ×3.00, Overtime ×1.50, Relief, NHS | unchanged | no evidence of a change | Not changed |

**How it works:**
- `PayTrackerConfig.PAY_RULE_HISTORY` holds dated rates with their evidence and `contractConfirmed: false`. `PAY_RULES` keeps the rate in force before any dated entry.
- One resolver (`getConfiguredPayRule_`) picks the rule for a shift's calendar day (Europe/London). Every pay path passes the PaySheet row's own date: Calendar sync, hand edits (`updatePayForRow`), and weekly / all-pay recalculation (`recalculateWeeklyTable`, `recalculateAllPay`). Anything called without a date gets the **historical** rate, so nothing is moved forward by accident.
- **Payroll week:** Monday to Sunday, week ending Sunday (as on the payslips). A night shift belongs to the date it starts, so a Sunday 4 Oct night is week ending 4 Oct and stays £20.21.
- **Effective date chosen:** 5 Oct 2026, the first day of the earliest payroll week seen paid at £20.87. The real contractual date can only be the same or later within that week, or earlier if Staffline paid the w/e 04/10 night at the old rate by mistake. **Ask Staffline:** (a) the exact date £20.87 took effect; (b) whether the Basic (£13.47) and the other multipliers changed too (£20.87 / 1.5 = £13.913 would imply Basic ≈ £13.91). Then add their answers to `PAY_RULE_HISTORY`.

**First sync after deploying:** calendar-owned Night ×1.50 rows dated from 5 Oct that nobody has edited are re-priced at £20.87, and ×1.33 rows at £17.92. Rows dated earlier keep their pay. Hand-corrected rows are never changed.

## 3. NHS placements and overtime: separate, and not combined
- **Hours are never combined across placements.** Each NHS timesheet is reconciled only with its own hospital's shifts, and NHS shifts count 7.5 paid hours (both fixed in PR #51). Combined *income* reporting is unchanged.
- **Overtime stays manual.** Staffline has confirmed a **37.5-hour** threshold. It has **not** been established whether hours from separate Antrim and Causeway assignments count together towards it. The only overtime evidence (Aug, N624186: 45h → 7.5h HSC Overtime) is a single timesheet. So Pay Tracker calculates no overtime automatically; existing manual overtime entries (HSC Overtime shift types) are unchanged and are protected as manual corrections. **Ask Staffline:** do hours on separate assignment timesheets (Antrim and Causeway) count together towards the 37.5-hour overtime threshold, and if so, which timesheet carries the overtime?

## 4. October reconciliation, reproduced with the engine
`Tests/node/OctoberPayroll_Scenarios.cjs` prices each correction with the real engine at its own date. Only the payslip totals and Night lines are known; the other payslip lines are not.

| Week ending | Original estimate | + missing NHS shift | + Night ×1.50 rate | Corrected | Payslip gross | Difference | Deductions | Net |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 11 Oct 2026 | £563.84 | 7 Oct Antrim 10–18: **£95.33** | 8h × (£20.87 − £20.21): **£5.28** | **£664.45** | £664.43 | **−£0.02** | £152.97 | £511.46 |
| 18 Oct 2026 | £719.08 | 15 Oct Antrim 08–16: **£95.33** | **£5.28** | **£819.69** | £819.67 | **−£0.02** | £216.80 | £602.87 |

- The engine reproduces all three Night payslip lines exactly: £90.95, £166.96 and £166.96, as one line or as two 4-hour PaySheet shifts (2 × £83.48).
- **Where the −£0.02 comes from: rounding, not a calculation error.**
  - PaySheet rounds each NHS shift on its own: 7.5 × £12.71 = £95.325 → £95.33, and the 14–22 split £105.745 → £105.75.
  - Staffline rounds once per payslip line: e.g. 30.00h × £12.71 = £381.30, where four PaySheet shifts add up to £381.32.
  - So four such NHS shifts in a week make the estimate exactly 2p high. Every such shift adds at most 0.5p.
  - The exact count for those weeks can be confirmed from the payslips' NHS lines, but no correction is needed.
- **Net pay:** the Payroll Centre's Payslip Register already stores actual Gross, Net, Tax, NI, Pension, Student Loan and Other deductions, plus Predicted Gross and Gross Difference. The Staffline parser extracts gross £664.43, deductions £152.97 and net £511.46 from the summary row (tested). The comparison engine reports **gross −£0.02 = MATCHED**, and shows actual net and deductions with predictions **NOT AVAILABLE**: Pay Tracker invents no tax, NI, pension or student-loan figures.
